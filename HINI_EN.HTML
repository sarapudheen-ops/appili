TOYOTA/LEXUS V10.62

Updates

  • Added support for the DTC, freeze frame, data stream, and actuation test functions of 2025MY Toyota models such as Harrier HV, Crown HEV, Century, Harrier, Camroad, Dyna, Crown FCEV, Fortuner/Hilux SW4, Hiace, Venza HV.
  • Added support for common special functions of 2025MY Toyota models such as Harrier HV, Crown HEV, Century, Harrier, Camroad, Dyna, Crown FCEV, Fortuner/Hilux SW4, Hiace, Venza HV.
  • Updated the service function of Toyota to 2025.
